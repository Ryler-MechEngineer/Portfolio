# Project Page Template

Copy this into `projects/project-name/README.md` for each new project. Keep the section order so every page reads the same way.

```markdown
# Project Name

**Role:** what you did, where, and when
**Tools:** CAD, software, fabrication methods
**Focus:** the 3 to 5 skills this project shows

## Summary
Two or three sentences. What it is, what you did, and the result.

## Objective
What the project had to achieve.

## What I did
Your specific contributions. On team projects, say which parts were yours.

## Key numbers
A small table of the specs that matter.

## The problem
What had to be solved, and the constraints (size, load, cost, time).

## Design decisions
For each major decision: what you chose, what else you considered, and why you chose it.

## Challenges
The hardest real problems, with specifics: what happened, how you found the cause, what you changed.

## What failed
What broke or underperformed. Say whether you expected it, what you found the cause to be, and why you picked the design that failed in the first place.

## How I fixed it
What you changed, and what the change did to the results.

## Result
Measured outcomes, with numbers where you have them.

## What I took from it
Lessons, in plain first-person language.
```

## Folder layout

```
projects/project-name/
  README.md
  images/   photos, CAD screenshots, diagrams
  cad/      STEP and STL exports (STL files preview in 3D on GitHub)
```

## Rules

- No confidential drawings, dimensions, or photos from internships or sponsors.
- Claim only what you did and what was tested. Say when something is simulation only.
