**Role:** Mechanical Engineering Intern, Houston Energy Corridor, summer 2026 (May to August)
**Tools:** SOLIDWORKS
**Focus:** Fixture design, root-cause analysis, QA/QC, process standardization

## Summary

I designed a new manufacturing fixture and worked with the engineering team on process updates, quality control, and troubleshooting. A manufacturing engineer's public LinkedIn recommendation credits my fixture and my broader process-improvement work with raising output by 50% and cutting the reported failure rate from 30% to 4%.

## Objective

Improve an existing manufacturing process through mechanical design, process updates, and quality control, without disrupting what production needed to keep running.

## What I did

- Designed a new fixture to improve process performance, repeatability, and output.
- Helped manufacturing engineers update and upgrade existing processes.
- Supported QA and QC work, CAD design, troubleshooting, and process improvement.
- Saw how an engineering concept becomes a production-ready, standardized process.
- Worked on shipping-container solutions in SOLIDWORKS and had exposure to ISO 9001 and lean practices.

## Design decisions

A fixture holds a part in the same position every time, so variation in the result comes from the process and not from how the part was held. I put the effort into workholding because that is where repeatability was being lost.
