## Summary

I designed new fixturing for a manufacturing process and worked with the engineering team on root-cause analysis, QA/QC, and corrective actions as part of manufacturing scale-up. A supervisor's public LinkedIn recommendation credits the fixturing change with raising output by 50% and cutting failures from about 30% to 4%.

## The problem and the decision

The goals for the fixture were repeatability and throughput. A fixture holds a part in the same position every time, so variation in the result comes from the process and not from how the part was held. I chose to attack the problem at the workholding step because that is where the repeatability was being lost.
