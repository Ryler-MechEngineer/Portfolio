# Part 1: The end cap holder

## Objective

Design a tube holder that stops end caps from falling away and chipping.

## What I did

- Ran a root-cause analysis to isolate what was causing the chipping.
- Designed a sponge-like holder printed in TPU.
- Helped manufacturing and ceramic engineering staff update and upgrade existing processes.
- Supported QA and QC work, CAD design, troubleshooting, and process improvement.
- Saw how an engineering concept becomes a production-ready, standardized process, and had exposure to ISO 9001 and lean practices.

## Design decisions

The process did not work before I got there, and I was told other engineers had already tried to fix it and could not. I treated the failures as a pattern to solve instead of one-off defects, and I focused on how the end caps were held.

I tested different TPU configurations until I found a sponge-like pattern. TPU is flexible, so the pattern deforms easily and does not fight the part, but it still holds the end cap in place.
