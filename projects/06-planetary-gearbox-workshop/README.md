# Robotic Arms 101 and the 3D-Printed Planetary Gearbox

**Role:** Presenter and builder, University Rover Challenge Arm Team onboarding
**Tools:** 3D printing
**Focus:** Gearbox types, torque, degrees of freedom, teaching mechanical fundamentals

## Summary

I built and gave a 15-minute onboarding presentation for the URC Arm Team. It covered what URC is, what the Arm Team does, degrees of freedom, torque, gearbox types, examples of real arms, and a first look at forward and inverse kinematics. After the talk, the attendees and I built a 3D-printed planetary gearbox together.
