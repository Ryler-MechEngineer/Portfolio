# Bluetooth-Controlled Battle Bot

**Role:** Team project, January to March 2025
**Tools:** SOLIDWORKS, Arduino, C++, Bluetooth
**Focus:** Custom armor, chassis and drivetrain, wireless drive control

## Summary

We built a small robot for sumo-style matches. I designed custom armor plating in CAD, programmed Bluetooth motor control and steering in Arduino and C++, and assembled the chassis, electronics, and drivetrain with attention to stability and pushing force.
