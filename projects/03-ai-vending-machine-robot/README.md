# AI-Powered Vending Machine Robot

**Role:** Engineering Team Lead, Rowdy Creators, November 2025 to May 2026
**Tools:** SOLIDWORKS, 3D printing
**Focus:** Mechanical system design, drivetrain layout, integration, mentoring

## Summary

I led the mechanical side of a mobile robot meant to navigate on its own and dispense items. I was responsible for the physical system: the drivetrain, the structure, and the platform that carries the upper components. I did not own the full electronics and software stack. The work ran on a development cycle of about three months.

## What I built and why

I designed the base mechanical system, custom parts in CAD, and the mounting platform for everything above the drivetrain, then supported fabrication and assembly. I also worked with the electrical and software teammates so parts fit together and wiring had room to run, and I coached teammates who were newer to design.

## What failed

PLA servo horns and other PLA components failed under load. The team moved to metal components where that made sense.

## Where it ended

The mechanical assembly was completed. Wiring integration and full-system testing were still ahead when the term ended, so I am not claiming a validated autonomous system here.

## What I took from it

Something working in CAD is not the same as something that survives real loads. PLA is good for checking geometry and fit, and parts that carry load need to be designed and made for it.
