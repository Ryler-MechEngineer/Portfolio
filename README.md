# Ryler Collins | Engineering Portfolio

Mechanical Engineering student at the University of Texas at San Antonio (Honors College), graduating May 2028. I design in CAD, build what I design, and write down what broke. I'm aiming at manufacturing, automation, and robotics.

Every project page follows the same order: the problem, the design decisions and why I made them, what failed, how I fixed it, and what I took from it. Failures are part of the record, not something I cut out.
